    About this project +

    The real build

    A published mobile Power Apps interface with Dataverse tables for ideas, reviews and listings. The Gelato product-read connection has been tested; product creation and Shopify draft automation remain in development.

    This experience

    A browser recreation of that workflow. The preview is drawn locally; product creation and listing steps are simulated. No AI service, store, payment system or private environment is contacted.

    What connects it

    Power Apps → Dataverse → Power Automate → review → Gelato → Shopify. Separate design, review and listing states keep each step visible.
