    About this project +

    The real build

    Linked idea and design-brief records, orientation rules, readiness states and SharePoint asset folders. Individual workflow stages have been developed and tested; the full ecommerce workflow remains in development.

    This experience

    A browser recreation using illustrative SVG artwork and fictional file specifications. Print requirements are calculated from your choices. The downloads are sample SVG and JSON files, not production-ready raster artwork or a published listing.

    What connects it

    Idea → design brief → approval → artwork → checks → output. Reference numbers and linked records keep the creative decisions connected to the asset.
